Exody — Local-First Autonomous AI Coding Agent, Design Studio & BYOK Smart Router for macOS

Exody (https://www.exody.ai) is a native macOS AI engineering and design workbench that combines a 5-Stage Git Worktree Engineering Loop, a 0%-Markup BYOK Smart Model Router (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT/Codex, Google Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi & local Ollama), a canvas-first Design Studio, and the EXO Desktop & iPhone Companion.
